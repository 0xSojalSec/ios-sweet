Open problems

What no project has solved yet, and what only one or two have. This is where the community's work is going, and where help is most useful. Nothing here is a promise or a date.

Metal for every app

Drawing the iOS interface on the GPU is solved in a few places. HackiOS, the Redmi Note 12 port and the Pixel (2016) port use OpenGL ES through Mesa's freedreno driver. The Pixel 4 port and UniXNU (Adreno 740, with its own native kernel GPU driver) run Metal by translating it to Vulkan for Mesa's Turnip driver.

What's open is Metal that's complete and fast enough for any app, on more than one GPU generation. Each Adreno generation needs its own work, and Apple's shaders have to be translated before the GPU can run them. HackiOS's notes on that are in docs/air.md.

Bluetooth

No project has Bluetooth yet. iOS expects something that behaves like Apple's own Bluetooth controller, and on most phones the Bluetooth chip shares hardware and firmware with Wi-Fi. The LG V60 port is working on its Wi-Fi and Bluetooth chip.

Calls, App Store and Apple ID

No project handles calls, texts or mobile data. App Store downloads and Apple ID sign-in need Apple's FairPlay DRM and the Secure Enclave, a security chip these phones don't have. This is the hardest category, and maybe one that never fully works.

Hardware that works on only one or two devices

  • Wi-Fi: HackiOS, and the Pixel (2016), Pixel 4 and Redmi Note 12 ports. Every Wi-Fi chip family needs its own driver.
  • Sound: HackiOS and the Redmi Note 12 port.
  • Camera: HackiOS only.
  • Battery level and charging: the Pixel (2016) and Redmi Note 12 ports. On most others the battery shows a fixed value or only drains.

Each of these is a good thing to port from one device to another, since the closest working driver is already open source.

Other gaps

  • Video and HEIC images. There's no hardware video decoder yet, so videos and HEIC photos don't open on several ports.
  • Heat. Several ports have no thermal management yet, so running the CPU at full speed makes the phone very hot, and they keep it at modest speeds by default.
  • Sleep. Real suspend and resume, so the phone saves power with the screen off, is missing almost everywhere.
  • Sensors and GPS. No project lists them as working yet.
  • Small CPU cores. Some phones' small cores can't use the 16 KB memory pages iOS expects. UniXNU and the Redmi Note 12 port solve this with a 4 KB-page kernel; other ports still leave those cores idle.
  • Newer iOS. The phone projects stop at iOS 16 because the newer iPhone firmware needs pointer authentication. Chips that have it (Snapdragon 8 Gen 1 and later, for example) could in theory run newer firmware, but no project does that yet.

More devices

New ports are the fastest way for all of this to grow. Community ports already boot on Pixels, Xiaomi phones, an LG and even an Exynos Galaxy S7. Start with the porting guide and share your progress on r/hackios.
